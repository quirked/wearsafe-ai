# F02 demo_v1 — reducing false prompts (PRELIMINARY · post-hoc but held-out)

**PRELIMINARY — demo sprint (tag `demo_v1`), 2026-10-12. Post-hoc but held-out.**
- The v0 results on all subjects had already been seen when these variants were designed.
- Parameters were chosen on a **dev** partition only. All numbers below are from the **test** partition (10 PPG-DaLiA people, 7 FallAllD people), which was not used for any choice.
- **Device caveat:** the motion model (trained on FallAllD) is applied to PPG-DaLiA's E4 watch data across a device gap (M01 device check: AUC 0.72 / 0.84). PPG-DaLiA is clipped at ±2 g, so false-alert rates may be optimistic for wider-range watches.
- Numbers come from:
  - `reports/F02_demo_v1_test_tables.md` [T§], the dev grid in `reports/F02_demo_v1_dev_tables.md`;
  - `results/2026-10-12_F02_demo_v1/` (all generated by `scripts/f02_demo.py`).

## Plain-language summary
1. **No new variant reaches the 3-prompts-per-hour target while keeping most falls.** On dev, no V1 setting reached ≤ 3/h, so every V1 variant used the fallback: the fewest prompts while still detecting ≥ 80% of dev falls.
2. **The best new variants cut V0-base's false prompts by about a third (−35% to −37%):**

   | Variant | False prompts/h (test) | Falls detected (test) |
   |---|---|---|
   | V0-base | 23.8 | 94.5% |
   | V1-tiered / V1-combined | 15.6 | 82.7% |
   | V1-change | 15.1 | 77.1% |

   - The cost is about 12–17 percentage points of fall detection.
   - Prompts that wait for the change check are delayed by 5 s.
3. **V0-stillness is still the only variant under 3/h** (2.4/h on test), but it detects only **49.8%** of falls.
   - By the pre-registered rule ("most falls among settings ≤ 3/h"), it is the officially recommended variant.
   - The best new (V1) variant is V1-change (fallback rule). Both statements are true; the choice between "few prompts" and "most falls" is a product decision, not something the data can settle here.
4. **V1-merge changed nothing.** As specified, it equals the existing 30 s refractory rule, so its numbers are identical to V0-base (a sanity check).
5. **The main remaining false-prompt sources** [T§2]:
   - table soccer stays at 75–77 prompts/h for every motion-based variant (20/h with stillness);
   - cycling stays at 52/h with change;
   - the change rule mostly removes stairs (16.5 → 3.3/h) and walking (32.6 → 11.3/h).

## What was pre-registered and how it was chosen
- **Partition** (`config/fusion_v1_partition.json`, `numpy.random.RandomState(2026)`):
  - PPG-DaLiA dev S01, S10, S12, S14, S15; test the other 10;
  - FallAllD dev S04, S05, S09, S12, S14, S15; test S01, S02, S03, S06, S10, S11, S13.
- **Variants and grids** (`config/fusion_v1.yaml`, written before any v1 evaluation):
  - **change:** the 5 s after the alarm vs the 10 s before it, relative change of |a| std or dominant frequency > tol ∈ {0.3, 0.5, 0.7};
  - **T_high:** dev fall-positive score quantiles (0.861 / 0.724 / 0.527).
- **Selection (dev only):** most falls subject to ≤ 3/h; otherwise the fallback.
- **Correction recorded before the test run** (`selection_correction.json`, PROJECT_LOG): my script picked the recommended variant among V1 variants only. The pre-registered rule covers all variants, which gives V0-stillness. Both are reported; `selection.json` was not overwritten.

## Code
- `src/wearsafe/fusion_v1.py`: the change measures (only data up to the decision time), the candidate rules and the decision with the 30 s refractory. It is a new file; `inference.py` is unchanged.
- `tests/test_fusion_v1.py`: streams PPGDALIA_S03 (a test subject) one second at a time. Each change decision is taken 5 s after its alarm, with only the samples available then. It reproduces the offline prompt rate of V1-change and V1-tiered exactly.

## Limitations / still to do
- **Post-hoc but held-out:** the variants were designed after seeing v0 on all subjects.
- **Small test partition:** 10 PPG-DaLiA and 7 FallAllD people. The 49.8% / 77–83% / 94.5% fall-detection figures come from 271 test fall clips.
- **Change detection near clip edges:** FallAllD clips are only 20 s and the change rule needs 10 s before and 5 s after the alarm. Detection may differ on continuous real-world data.
- **Position test (b)** is still owed before any non-demo claim (F01 "Still to do").
- **Context-based suppression** (table soccer, cycling, driving) was not part of v1; it is the obvious next pre-registered candidate.
